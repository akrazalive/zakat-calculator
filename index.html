Privacy Policy

Zakat Calculator · Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how the Zakat Calculator Android app ("the app") handles information. It is published by the app developer ("we").

Information you enter

All amounts, prices and calculation history that you enter are stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit or store this information on any server. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and information about ad interactions. Please see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings & Info → Ad privacy choices. On any Android device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
